About Us

Paws and Claws Rescue is a nonprofit organization that is centered around finding as many homes for our animals as we can.

We are a no kill shelter which means we do not use euthanasia on any of our animals unless it is absolutely necessary for medical reasons. We believe every animal that comes to us deserves a loving home.

Our mission is to not only find a home for all our pets but to be able to help find homes for as many dogs and cats as we possibly can.

How To Get Involved

The number one way that most people get involved is by adopting one of our lovely animals! We have a list of dogs and cats currently available for adoption and even offer playdates with any animal in order for both the pet and family to get to know each other better.

While we encourage anyone is who able and willing to adopt a pet we also understand that this is not feasible for everyone. Therefore, there are many other ways to support us and our furry friends.

As a no kill shelter, we take pride in giving each one of our animals the same care and love, however this also means we require more resources to take care of so many animals. Therefore, we are always accepting donations to help us run this shelter and be able to rescue as many dogs and cats as we can. Every donation goes to making sure all of our animals have the best care.

We also welcome volunteers to help feed the animals and walk them!
